    Method

    Leaf-only crop. Each photo is cropped to the largest square that contains only leaf, so the background can't decide the answer. Our first models learned exactly that shortcut.

    Input check. A pretrained EfficientNet-B0 compares the photo with four reference groups: corn rust or healthy, other corn diseases, other crops, and non-plant photos. Only photos closest to the first group go on to the classifier.

    Classifiers. SmallCNN (0.39M parameters, trained from scratch), MobileNetV3-Small and EfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet, fine-tuned), trained on two corn photo sources. The ensemble averages SmallCNN and MobileNetV3. Held-out test accuracy: SmallCNN 99.7%, MobileNetV3 99.4%, EfficientNet-B0 98.5%.

    Heatmap. Grad-CAM marks the regions that pushed the prediction.

    Photos are analyzed in memory and never stored.

    Limits

    • Close-up leaf photos only. Whole plants, crowded fields and distant leaves are outside what the models learned.
    • Two classes. Our models know common rust and healthy. Other corn diseases are usually rejected by the input check, but not always.
    • The input check can be wrong in both directions.
    • The heatmap is not severity. It shows influence on the prediction, not how sick the leaf is.
    • Not field-tested yet. Test photos come from existing datasets, not new field photos.
